Dayboard Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Operator (developer): Jaeha Lee

Dayboard is a personal tasks, calendar and notes app for Windows. It has no server, no sign-up and no ads. Data from the accounts you connect is stored on your PC (only your notes, when notes sync is on, and your list and calendar view settings are also kept with that account). This policy explains what information Dayboard handles and why.

1. Information Dayboard handles

Connected accountWhat Dayboard getsWhat it's used for
GoogleAccount email and profile name (openid, email, profile)Showing which account is connected
Google Tasks lists and tasks (tasks, read and edit)Showing, adding, editing and completing tasks
Google Calendar calendar list and events (calendar.readonly, read; calendar.events, add, edit and delete events)Showing, adding, editing and deleting events
Dayboard's own app data folder in Google Drive (drive.appdata, read and write to this folder only)Syncing notes (including earlier versions and recently deleted notes) and list and calendar view settings with Dayboard on your other PCs
MicrosoftAccount information, Microsoft To Do lists and tasks (read and edit), Outlook calendar events (read and edit)Showing, adding, editing and deleting tasks and events; keeping list and calendar view settings as Dayboard's own data on the default To Do list, to match them with your other PCs
Apple (iCloud)The Apple Account email and app-specific password you enter, iCloud calendar events (read and edit)Showing, adding, editing and deleting events

If you don't connect an account, Dayboard handles no account information at all. Tasks and notes you type into the app are likewise stored only on your PC.

  • Notes sync runs only when a Google account is connected. You can turn it off with the Notes switch on the Accounts screen in the app.
  • To match them between PCs that use the same accounts, list and calendar view settings (a list's colour, icon, order, group name, hidden state and sort; calendar and event colours) are kept in the app data folder of a Google account with this permission and on the default To Do list of a connected Microsoft account. They hold no task, event or note content, and they stop when you disconnect the account, whatever the Notes switch says.
  • Only Dayboard can access its app data folder. The folder doesn't show up in Drive or in other apps.
  • Dayboard never sees or changes any other file in your Drive.

2. Where data is stored and for how long

  • Sign-in tokens and app-specific passwords are stored only in Windows encrypted storage on your PC (an encrypted file in the app data folder).
  • Tasks, events and notes Dayboard fetches are stored in the app data folder on the same PC.
  • With notes sync on, your notes (including earlier versions and recently deleted notes) are kept on your PC and also in the app data folder of that Google account's Drive. List and calendar view settings are kept in those two places too, besides your PC.
  • That folder's contents belong to your Google account. Once you disconnect in the app, or revoke Dayboard's access in your Google Account, Dayboard no longer accesses the folder.
  • Dayboard runs no server. It never sends, sells or shares this information with the developer or any third party, and never uses it for analytics or advertising.
  • Information moves only between each service (Google, Microsoft, Apple) and your PC, using each service's official API over HTTPS.
  • When checking for updates, Dayboard downloads only new-version information and installers from Dayboard's releases on GitHub. No user data is sent.
  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes that account's sign-in information immediately. Lists and events already fetched stay as a local copy, which you can delete in the app.

3. Your rights and how to revoke access

  • Disconnect any time from the Accounts screen in the app.
  • Google: revoke permissions at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Microsoft: revoke permissions under Apps and services in your Microsoft account.
  • Apple: delete the app-specific password at account.apple.com.
  • Uninstalling the app leaves its app data folder (%APPDATA%\Dayboard) in place. Deleting that folder too removes all information stored on the PC.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Dayboard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
